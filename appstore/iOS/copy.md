# App Store Copy - Amateur Radio Log (iOS)

## Promotional Text

Log contacts, track progress, and sync across all your devices. Built for amateur radio operators who want a clean, native iOS experience with QRZ.com, HamQTH, and LoTW integration.

## Description

Amateur Radio Log is a native iPhone and iPad amateur radio logging application with iCloud sync, interactive maps, and integration with the services you already use.

**Log your contacts**
Record QSOs with full ADIF field support including band, mode, frequency, signal reports, grid squares, and more. Auto-lookup callsigns via QRZ.com or HamQTH to fill in operator details instantly. Your last-used band, mode, frequency, and power are remembered automatically.

**See your contacts on a map**
Every contact with a grid square or coordinates appears on an interactive map. Color pins by band, mode, or signal strength. Filter by time range, band, or mode. Tap any pin to see every QSO with that station.

**Track your progress**
See how many countries, US states, and unique callsigns you've worked. View QSO breakdowns by band and mode. Find your furthest contact and your weakest signal report. Filter stats by band, mode, or date range. Tap any stat to jump to the matching log entries.

**Sync with QRZ.com, HamQTH, and LoTW**
Upload new contacts and download QSOs you've logged elsewhere. LoTW integration downloads your QSL confirmations and marks confirmed contacts automatically. Bi-directional sync with QRZ.com and HamQTH with automatic deduplication.

**Sync across devices**
Your log, settings, and last-used defaults sync automatically across iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud. Log a contact in the field and review it on your Mac at home.

**Import and export**
Import and export standard ADIF files. Export POTA-formatted logs for Parks on the Air activation uploads.

**Powerful search and filtering**
Search across callsigns, names, locations, countries, and comments. Filter by band, mode, date range, country, state, grid prefix, CQ zone, ITU zone, continent, or county. Tap any value in the log to instantly filter by it.
